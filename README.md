## HOW TO APPLY

Applications are **GITHUB ISSUES or PULL REQUESTS**. One per candidate.
No email. No DMs. No back door.

Answers, code, portfolio — put it in a **GIST or EXTERNAL LINK** and reference
it in the thread. The thread stays a thread.

### ROUTE A — ISSUE

Open an issue with the **Crew application** template. Prove you solved the
quest. Sign your handle.

[OPEN AN ISSUE →](https://github.com/underground-software-engineers-nyc/underground-engineer-NYC-job-board/issues/new/choose)

### ROUTE B — PULL REQUEST

Fork the repo. Add `applications/<your-handle>.md`. Open the PR with the
template. The crew reads the diff.

[FORK + OPEN A PR →](https://github.com/underground-software-engineers-nyc/underground-engineer-NYC-job-board/fork)

## WHAT WE READ

The template asks for three things: **proof of the signal** (you solved the
quest — the channel proves it), **what you build** (real systems, shipped —
links beat claims), and **where you ride** (your discipline and the line you
want to run). One idea per answer. The crew reads every submission.

## THE DECISION

- Every submission gets stamped `review` and is read by the crew.
- `accepted` — you join the private crew repo. A signed pass is minted with your handle inside it.
- `refused` — polite, one line. The tunnel keeps its counsel.

## WHAT MEMBERSHIP MEANS

Acceptance is not a badge. It is a working position inside the operation.

**You print your own artifacts.** Accepted engineers mint and print their own
signed UGE QR stickers — the same self-verifying artifact system the crew runs
— and distribute them across the city. Your stickers carry your handle inside
the token. Every scan verifies the signature, lands on the decoder, and credits
the artifact to you. The city reads your work off the walls.

**Work finds you.** Clients and crew come through the hall. Client briefs are
posted here as `job-application` issues — members bid, the operator routes the
work. Anyone in the group can contact you for a job; anyone who scans one of
your artifacts in the field is one transfer away from your handle. Your field
presence *is* your business card.

**You hold a signed pass.** A cryptographic crew pass is minted with your
handle inside — it opens the private crew repo and the hiring hall. Revocable,
like every artifact in this system. Keep the work honest.

**You run the system, not just ride it.** The crew ships its own
infrastructure — signed artifacts, static IPFS sites, self-hosted link engine.
Members build and operate the machine together. AI generates. Engineers decide.
